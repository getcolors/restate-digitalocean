Restate on DigitalOcean

A single-node durable workflow deployment at bigconfig.website.

Architecture

Cloudflare and Caddy expose HTTPS. Restate ingress, admin, fabric, metrics and the SDK endpoint remain private on one Amsterdam Droplet.

Recovery

Persistent disk survives restarts and daily R2 archives support manual recovery. This single-node design is not highly available.
